**Bet or Book Audit - September 19, 2026**

My conclusion: the project is worth carrying through to submission. It has a clear task, a working site and a substantive Nansen integration. But in its current form it explains trading intentions too confidently from incomplete observations. The main work before the contest is to fix the reliability of the conclusions, protect the budget and turn the result into understandable evidence that people want to share.

The most significant finding: four cards in the saved gallery attribute to the traders being checked the assets of the exchange wallets that funded them. This is an already observable data error in the product. It matters more than adding new screens, AI models or trading mechanics.

**What was checked.** Only Bet or Book, as clarified by the user. The code at commit `5187ca6`, the whole of `src`, the interface HTML/JavaScript, the configuration, the collection scripts, the existing tests, fixtures, README, the project specification and the demo script; the local gallery and ledger; the published site; official Nansen and Cloudflare materials. On the site, gallery loading, opening a card, the mobile size, one live request and the copy-image button were checked. The live request completed successfully: on September 19 at 12:53 UTC the card showed an ETH short of roughly $189.8M, 7 Nansen calls, linked assets 232%. The 108 existing tests pass; `tsc --noEmit` passes; a fresh `npm audit` showed 0 known vulnerabilities. **A01. An exchange's assets are not its client's hedge.** In [normalize.ts](../../src/sources/normalize.ts#L109) a shared service is determined by a regular expression over a single `address_label`. The absence of a label is treated as the absence of a service. Then [readLinkedHedge](../../src/api/check.ts#L209) admits the First Funder into the calculation.

In the actual data:

Public labels: [Binance 15 in the Etherscan explorer](https://mega.etherscan.io/address/0x21a31ee1afc51d94c2efccaa2092ad1028285549), [Gate Deposit on Etherscan](https://etherscan.io/address/0x0d0707963952f2fba59dd06f2b425ace40b492fe?age=1H). Minimal fix: exclude the confirmed service addresses and recalculate the affected cards. Full fix: a `service / non-service / unverified` state, aggregation of checks by address across all chains, a conservative treatment of missing labels. A regex must not turn the unknown into proven ownership. A First Funder without additional evidence should yield "the funding address has matching assets", not automatically probable hedge. This cannot be solved merely by adding two addresses to a blacklist.

**A02-A03. The structure model and neutrality are different questions.** [verdict.ts](../../src/engine/verdict.ts#L149) merges same-asset holdings and balanced book into a single Hedged. But a $1M BTC long plus a $1M TRUMP short do not cancel out the risk of each asset. Currently this example receives Hedged without additional data. Dollar net/gross is acceptable as a characteristic of the portfolio, but not as proof of neutrality.

The 50% threshold means only partial compensation. From a $1M short and $0.5M spot, a $0.5M short remains. The reverse example: a $1M short and $2.32M spot under common ownership and with no other legs leave $1.32M long. So the wordings "not a directional view", "neutral on price" and "carry trade" do not follow from the 232% figure. For linked wallets, the ownership itself and the allocation of these assets between strategies have not yet been proven.

Recommendation: describe `same-asset offset`, `partial offset`, `long/short portfolio`, `possible linked exposure` separately. Show the source amounts and the residual visible exposure where it is actually computable. Present linked assets separately from own coverage, without mechanically adding them into a "reliable" percentage. The assessment applies to the selected position, not to the wallet as a whole. In the live example the ETH short is only 39% of gross; its explanation does not classify the remaining positions.

**A04. No data is not the same as no hedge.** [check.ts](../../src/api/check.ts#L130) records errors in `coverage`, but [computeVerdict](../../src/engine/verdict.ts#L163) does not receive this field. In a test failure of all cross-chain/linked reads, Looks like a bet is returned. With `complete:false`, `hedgeScope: all-chains` is also returned, and the summary says that ETH was not found "on any chain". The page lists the limitation in small print, but the main conclusion stays the same.

For funders, `complete` is not taken into account at all during normalization. For related wallets only the first page is read. The absence of pagination in the client is treated as `complete:true`. The current-balance documentation provides for pagination and token symbol/address filters: they can be applied in a targeted way while keeping spending under control. [Endpoint contract](https://docs.nansen.ai/api/profiler/address-current-balances).

Fix: structured completeness per source, distinct `missing / partial / complete / not-applicable`, a ban on conclusions about the absence of a hedge under missing/partial, a clear "directional exposure is visible; the hedge could not be verified". If the budget does not allow the page to be read to the end, that is a valid limited result, not a zero balance.

**A05-A06. The financial limit and request protection.** credits.ts does a read-modify-write after all the requests. [index.ts](../../src/index.ts#L77) only checks the previous total before doing the work. There is no reservation. Even a single request at 299 of 300 can spend another 7; parallel requests receive permission simultaneously and overwrite the counter. Offline reproduction: 20 checks admitted, 140 new calls, 439 in total, 306 saved. This is a model of possible interleaving, not a measurement of production spend.

[KVRateLimiter](../../src/guard.ts#L60) is not atomic either. Besides the race, Cloudflare allows no more than one write to a single KV key per second; writes from several completed checks to the shared daily key conflict. KV is eventually consistent. These restrictions follow from the [write documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/api/write-key-value-pairs/) and the [KV model](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/concepts/how-kv-works/).

`safeKv` successfully disables Nansen if the error occurred before the budget check. But it cannot retroactively prevent paid calls already made if the final write failed. On every request the limiter writes even on a cache hit, because it is invoked before the cache. If Free KV with a daily limit of 1000 writes is used, at 20 sequential requests per minute to a single cached address about 50 minutes are enough to exhaust that quota. The account's specific plan was not checked. The behavior after a failure is a mass switch to HL-only, which degrades the main contest scenario.

Build separate atomic budget accounting: reserve the maximum cost before the request, then confirm the actual spend, and release only the reserve that is safely unused. On Workers, coordination through a Durable Object is suitable; keep KV for results. Move request limiting to the standard Rate Limiting binding or a similar edge guard. Rate Limiting should not be used as the bookkeeping for the global budget either: it has local semantics. [Binding documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/runtime-apis/bindings/rate-limit/).

Additionally, deduplication of simultaneous checks of an address is needed: the current [withCache](../../src/cache.ts#L5) launches the producer ten times on ten parallel cache misses. On Workers, a request-owned fetch promise cannot be carelessly moved into global state; coordination is better kept in the same place where the launch of the work is controlled.

**A07-A09. Which actions exactly count as evidence.** The trades signal contains only the number of fills, the share of buys and the share of taker. It has no notional, no market identity, no interval of the sample actually captured. So 300 small maker fills on another market can reclassify a large standalone ETH position as Book and stop the search for a hedge. In the gallery 12 of the 24 book cards triggered exclusively on trades. This is a group for re-checking, not proof that all 12 are wrong.

Recommendation: separate the account's activity profile from the property of the selected position. Separate spot/perps/dex, keep trade sizes, distinguish the execution strategy of a large order from sustained two-sided liquidity, show the span of the sample. 2000 fills may cover minutes; the share of buys within them does not guarantee a description of the whole day. And the number of orders without size/distance to mid can easily be distorted by small far-away orders.

Nansen provides HIP-3 positions, but the [Hyperliquid client](../../src/sources/hyperliquid.ts#L151) calls frontendOpenOrders without dex. That is a request for the first perp dex, including spot; it does not prove the absence of orders on the other markets. Fetch orders for the dexes of the open positions or explicitly lower the completeness of the classification. [Hyperliquid: frontendOpenOrders](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/Hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint), [perp dex endpoints](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint/perpetuals).

[normalizeNansenBalances](../../src/sources/normalize.ts#L103) discards token_address. After that, [aliases](../../src/engine/assets.ts#L5) trust the symbol alone. The reproduction accepts an arbitrary contract with the ticker WETH as ETH. `hide_spam_token` is useful but does not replace an asset identifier. A registry of chain + contract/token-id + underlying is needed, with a policy for unrecognized assets.

Positive aTokens/LST balances by themselves do not give net exposure: the model does not know the borrowed assets, liabilities and other positions of these funders. It is especially dangerous to call such a result delta neutral. An additional `portfolio/defi-holdings` is potentially useful precisely here, because it contains assets/debts, and not for increasing the number of integrations. Until it is added, explicitly limit the conclusion. [Nansen Portfolio API](https://docs.nansen.ai/api/portfolio).

**A10-A12. Resilience to API responses.** HTTP success currently equals valid data. The reproduction `perpPositions → {}` ended in a TypeError although the HL fallback is available: normalization is performed outside the rejected-branch handler. Runtime validation of shape, enums, finiteness of numbers and required arrays is needed, plus a fallback when the contract is violated. `Number(...)` does not check NaN/Infinity; a TypeScript cast does not check JSON.

On a timeout no record of the call appears: [nansen.ts](../../src/sources/nansen.ts#L100) invokes the recorder only after fetch. The call may have been processed by the server and charged. An attempted/unknown outcome, a reserve and subsequent reconciliation are needed. On 401/402/403 the cutoff happens only for future requests after recordCalls; the current check continues with other stages. In the reproduction this produced five requests on the same 402. Introduce a request-level circuit breaker that admits only calls already started.

One missing free source currently rejects the entire first Promise.all, although, for example, the inability to fetch OI should not break the main result. Sources should have critical/optional tiers; a partial result is acceptable with an honestly weakened conclusion. An overall deadline is needed on top of the timeouts of individual stages: four sequential paid stages can take about 80 seconds plus the free reads.

`checkedAt` marks the start of the check, not the time of the data. The Nansen timestamp is not used; in the reproduction an ancient timestamp is accepted without a warning. Add fetchedAt/dataAsOf where the source provides them, cache age, duration, completeness, classifierVersion. Mark snapshot and live read separately. A cache keyed by address alone keeps results of the old rules version after a deploy until the TTL expires, including degraded results: include the schema/classifier version and a reasonably short TTL for transient failures.

**A13-A15. Share, come back, don't mix up.** The Copy link button builds `/?address=...`; a new visitor performs a new check. This is a link to the address, not to the same result. After a change in the position, the rules or a budget fallback it may show a different conclusion. Verifying posts requires reproducible evidence: an immutable snapshot id, its time/version/sources, a separate refresh button. A link to a snapshot must work without paying for a repeated API call.

[drawCard](../../web/index.html#L406) does not carry over coverage and the permanent limitations. The static Book headline asserts market-making even at strength likely; Hedged asserts the absence of a directional view. The whole point of the careful caveats can be lost in the public image. The PNG must preserve at least the confidence level, completeness, time and one key limitation. For a probable link, that caveat should sit next to the main number.

There is a risk of long strings overlapping: `fillText(item.value, x, 478)` without maxWidth/wrapping in four columns. This risk was established by reading the code; the raster layout of the PNG was not fully checked in the audit. The Copy card button in the browser showed Copied, but the image turned out not to be accessible through a clipboard reader, so the visual success of the export is not asserted. Checks of a long HIP-3 symbol, long evidence and errors/partial data are needed, plus an explicit Download PNG.

The HTML lacks Open Graph/Twitter metadata with the result image. For the X audience this is an omission: the link does not produce a controlled card with evidence. Add a public snapshot page and a static image/metadata. Do not force the crawler to run JS and a paid check.

`runCheck` disables only the Check button. Enter and Check live can initiate competing requests. An old response can overwrite a new one, and after an error the old successful content remains. A request id/AbortController, a shared busy-state and an explicit status for the old result are needed. After selecting from the gallery the input field is not updated; synchronize the current address. The full address and the selected coin should be shown directly next to the result, not left only in the input/explorer link.

**A16-A18. Numbers and the sample.** [buildSpotPriceIndex](../../src/sources/normalize.ts#L52) uses the markPx of the last pair for the base symbol as USD. The quote token is ignored. The record contains USDC, USDT0, USDH and USDE. As long as they are around $1, the error may be small; when the rate deviates it changes the hedgeRatio. The synthetic UETH/UBTC test shows the structural problem: 0.03 is interpreted as $0.03 instead of $3000. Such a specific BTC quote was not observed in the saved fixture. A correct calculation requires the quote currency and conversion to USD, or the selection of a verified dollar pair by an explicit rule.

`headlineLiqDistancePct` in [features.ts](../../src/engine/features.ts#L39) is computed from entryPx. If the distance to liquidation is to be displayed now, the base must be the current mark. For example, entry=100, mark=150, liquidation=90: the code gives 10%, while the distance from mark is 40%. The field is currently not shown in the UI, so this is a defect of the API/a future feature, not an observable error in a visible indicator.

The gallery is 278 checks on September 18, 14:13-14:57 UTC, 277 open positions. Candidate selection first takes the top 3000 by accountValue from the public leaderboard, then ranks main-dex positions. A HIP-3-only wallet can be excluded before Nansen; leverage also breaks the link between accountValue and position size. At the end of the scan the budget favors cheaper long checks. So the phrase "49% of the largest positions are real bets" is a description of the selected slice and the current heuristics, not an assessment of the market. The right now headline is factually incorrect; under heavy traffic the saved cards are not refreshed.

Fix: curated cases + an honestly dated research sample; separately the number checked, the universe, the omissions, the selection method. For a live list, a limited refresh of the most needed cases within a budget. Do not rerun all 278 unnecessarily. In the research, explicitly separate accounts, positions and strategies.

**A19-A20. Preparing the submission and quality checking.** In the [demo script](../../docs/demo-script.md#L23) the prepared X text contains only workers.dev. Add GitHub to the post/submission itself as the FAQ requires. The repository [Sofiia7/bet-or-book](https://github.com/Sofiia7/bet-or-book) is publicly accessible. The form, the video and the post were not submitted in this audit.

A rehearsal with the cache is acceptable as a stability check, but the recording must show the real dataAsOf; a cached result should not be called a fresh request if no request took place. Before recording, check one important real case in advance, then record only after the errors described have been fixed.

The three calibration addresses are smoke tests. The one bet example was chosen by its observed shape, not by confirmed knowledge of all the legs; Abraxas is an indirect link; the thresholds were changed after looking at the sample itself. This is not an independent precision check. A separate table of 30-60 diverse cases is needed: directed, mixed, same-asset partial/full, multi-strategy, service funder, no label, API failure, HIP-3, empty. Independently label what can be confirmed, allowing "unknown", pin the version, measure above all false hedge/book and the abstention rate. Do not introduce "confidence 93%" without calibration.

The existing tests cover the branches of the current rules well, but some tests lock in disputed assumptions, for example balanced_book ⇒ hedged. There are no router/runtime/browser tests. `vitest` uses Node, FakeKV does not reproduce the platform's distribution and rate limits; scripts and the frontend JS are not in the current `tsc` scope. When fixing, test the observable behavior rather than simply changing the expected label to fit the new formula.

**Security: what was confirmed and what to strengthen.** A user-supplied URL is not fetched: the address is extracted, the outbound hosts are fixed. Strings are output through textContent; no dangerous HTML sinks were found in the current interface. There is no key in the client code. `.dev.vars` and `.env*` are excluded; these files were not found in the accessible git history, and a pattern search for literal secrets in the sources checked found nothing. This is not proof of the absence of any secret in all possible artifacts. There is no SQL, sessions, logins, uploads or execution of user code; standard attacks on these surfaces do not apply to the project.

CSP, nosniff, frame-ancestors and safe external links are present. `unsafe-inline` in script-src weakens the protection in case of future HTML injection; improve with a static hash/external script. Since no current XSS sink was found, this is P3 hardening, not a proven XSS. Observability logs/traces are not configured in wrangler.toml; add structured error/source/latency codes and budget guard events. Do not log keys and full upstream bodies.

Other specific improvements: an API method allowlist and a free HEAD; JSON error handling on all routes; Retry-After on 429; validation of numeric env thresholds with a refusal on NaN; an input length limit. `extractAddress` currently truncates a 66-character transaction hash to the first 42 characters and checks a different address - this was reproduced. Hex-token boundaries, handling of several addresses and explicit confirmation of the selected address are needed. SSRF does not follow from this, but the user may get an analysis of the wrong object.

**Data rights.** The current idea relies on suitable profiler endpoints, and linked wallets are accompanied by attribution. Nansen permits their publication under the stated conditions; labels and a number of Smart Money/leaderboard endpoints have separate restrictions. Do not add a public "top Smart Money" just because the endpoint is accessible with the key. Publication of raw labels, samples of smart-money wallets and derived results must comply with the specific conditions. The check here is limited to the public guidelines, not a legal opinion. [Official redistribution rules](https://docs.nansen.ai/guides/redistribution-guide).

**How I would reframe the product.** The promise "Should you copy this whale?" implies a recommendation that the current data does not provide: even a genuine directional bet can be a bad entry point. Better: "A whale opened a huge position. What are you actually looking at?" or "Check the position behind the headline". The Russian-language sense: "Understand the whale's position before drawing a conclusion from it".

The primary user is a Crypto Twitter/Hyperliquid reader who has seen a specific loud short/long; the second is an author/analyst who needs a verifiable card; the third is a person tracking changes in an already studied position. There is no need to turn the interface into a general screener for all these people at once.

A simple complete scenario: the user pastes an address, a link containing an address or the text of a claim → selects the coin mentioned → sees the position, its time slice and limitations → expands two or three reasons → gets a pinned card and a link to the evidence. An ordinary X link contains a tweet id, not a wallet: the current parser will not handle it. Before the deadline, honestly support text/address and suggest specifying the wallet explicitly; a full fetch of X posts is a separate integration, not a promised feature.

The graph does not have to be 3D. Two nodes, the direction of the transfer, a caption "a link does not prove ownership" and the exact asset are more useful than complex animation. For A01 an explicit answer "this transfer came from a service, its reserves are excluded" is needed - this can become a strong contest example of the tool checking its own errors.

Guess → reveal must not be scored as guessing the true intentions of an unknown owner. One can ask whether the data shown is sufficient for a conclusion, and give a walkthrough. Do not add monetary bets or a profitability leaderboard.

**What to remove or postpone.** Remove the claims "there is nothing to copy", "not a directional view" without sufficient grounds, "neutral carry trade" derived from a 200%+ balance. Remove right now from the static gallery. Move the contest call counter to About/README: it does not help the end user answer the question. In particular, a market maker must not be compared with a solo trader by it.

Do not add before submission: auto-trading, wallet connection and signing, a universal chat, dozens of networks, paid access, a referral mechanic, a complex points system, an arbitrary confidence score. Do not rewrite the project in React/Next for the contest: the current stack is suitable. Accurate data, a quality scenario and export are needed, not a new frontend architecture.

**Proposed result model.** It separates three independent things: observed exposure, activity pattern, data coverage. For example: "ETH short $X; $Y ETH found in own assets; two funding addresses hold $Z of potentially linked assets; ownership not established; only the selected position was examined". The badge can stay short, but must not erase this distinction. Within five seconds the user should understand what is established, what is assumed and what is missing.

Recommended minimum set of fields: snapshotId, classifierVersion, the selected positionId/coin/dex, observedAt per source, freshness/cache age, per-source coverage, own coverage, linked assets, the grounds for accepting/rejecting funders, reason codes. Do not include labels from closed sources in the public object. Build the API, the page, the PNG and the share metadata from this same object so that they say the same thing.

**Economics and operations.** The README justifies every paid check, but PnL is always requested and does not take part in the verdict. It is a candidate for a separate cache/lazy loading. The shared spot/perp metadata is currently downloaded for every address - cache it separately for a short time. Do not lose accuracy for the sake of savings: if a request is skipped, that is reflected in coverage.

Assuming 1 credit/call and an average of 3.2 calls/check, a limit of 300 gives roughly 93 fresh checks per day; at 7 calls, about 42. This is a capacity estimate, not a pricing promise: the cost depends on the plan, the headers and the endpoint. The documentation distinguishes Free/Pro prices, so the fallback "no header ⇒ 1 credit" is not universally conservative. Check the real spend and reserve at the applicable maximum rate. [Nansen pricing](https://docs.nansen.ai/getting-started/credits).

Product metrics: the share of users completing a first check, time to a useful result, expansion of evidence, creation of a share snapshot, returns to a snapshot, the share of unknown/incomplete answers, cost per fresh check. Quality metrics: false hedge/book on an independent set, the share of excluded service funders, source completeness. Do not collect unnecessary user identifiers for the sake of these metrics.
