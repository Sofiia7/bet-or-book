## Additional changes

- Positions that are 15 minutes or more away from the original check, or have a timestamp more than a minute in the future, are not mixed with holdings/orders as a consistent measurement. This is Unknown with a separate reason and dataQuality; reopening yesterday's consistent snapshot does not make it erroneous.
- The main dex cache does not declare a position absent on all venues. A free conclusion of absence requires a full roster and full venue coverage. Old shortened lists remain supported.
- Card: subject of the reading and address → date/age → conclusion → two facts → Share/Save → compact leverage/liquidation → open question → chart → full analysis. The repeat form is hidden until Check another. Detailed vitals and the comparison remain in Full analysis.
- In the user interface, "Hedged" is shown as "Spot-covered short". The Book headline describes evidence; the grade is not passed off as a probability. Long shows that spot coverage is not applicable instead of 0%.
- The chart has dollar amounts and a note that it illustrates coverage and is not a transaction graph. The wallet's own holdings and funders are explicitly distinguished.
- Saved positions store asset, side, snapshot and date. Open saved is free; Refresh is started manually. Old address arrays are read without losing the list. There is no promise of automatic monitoring.
- All read positions are available: the first five and a search across the full list. An old incomplete roster does not prove the absence of a sixth position.
- Boards use current comparable readings. Covered shorts are sorted by closeness to the short size; this is not a safety ranking. An empty modern Book board is not replaced with history.
- The main readings have a client-side timeout: 15 seconds for saved/compare/gallery, 55 seconds for live. The timeout does not cancel the server-side accounting of credits already spent; a repeat POST is not sent automatically.
- When the budget/provider is unavailable, the limited nature of the reading itself and the path to free examples are visible. A useless immediate retry is not offered when credits are off.
- Pitch/demo are aligned with the real three contrasts. The fourth example shows the limit of maker flow, not a nonexistent current Book. Team/location require the founder's own data.
- The three main examples got questions before opening; the fourth is in More evidence cases. The card gives an evidence takeaway, and Unknown for funders/partial spot/overcoverage shows the relevant amounts and the boundary of the conclusion.
- Several addresses in an alert require a choice before POST; duplicate addresses are merged. A hint was added about the wallet link and that X URLs are not parsed.
- After 8 seconds of waiting a free example is available. A late response does not replace the selected reading. The budget-off warning and recovery were checked on isolated fixtures without paid requests.
- Saved positions got names; a newly saved live reading updates the date/link of the same position and keeps the name. An older date does not overwrite a newer one.
- Explore starts with Recent case studies; the comparison, the full list and the archive open separately. The repeated provider lead was removed from the first level of the card; for the funder case, the wallet's own spot coverage is shown next to it.
- Save sits next to Share. The confirmation explains storage in this browser, gives the position name, copying the saved link and a jump to the list. Copy reading carries over the facts, the original date, the open question, attribution and the link; the X draft keeps the date and the limitation when the headline is shortened. A snapshot that failed to save does not get an invented link.
- After a manual refresh of a saved position, the previous user snapshot is kept. The comparison was moved out of Full analysis, gives a free link back, and explicitly describes either the absence of material changes or the unavailability of the comparison. Switching the question is not shown as a change in the trade; an old or unsaved response does not move the baseline. This is a manual comparison, not alerts.
