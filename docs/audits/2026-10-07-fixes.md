The classifier still withholds Bet/Hedged when the data required for those claims is incomplete. No threshold was relaxed merely to reduce Unknown counts (3.1-3.2). Seventeen unknown reasons are evidence distinctions, not seventeen bugs. Existing historical readings are never represented as fresh checks.

Funding estimates use oracle-valued size and the current hourly rate, held constant for 24 hours; they are estimates, not guaranteed carry. [Hyperliquid funding mechanics](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/funding) and [perp metadata API](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint/perpetuals) define the inputs. Fills totals cover the actual sample, which can be shorter than a day or capped by the API. Main-dex account metrics do not describe collateral on every dex.

Remaining product experiments: empirically calibrated quote distance/age thresholds, aggregate guessing statistics, automatic refresh scheduling and outreach validation. These are optional additions in the audit, not repairs to deployed behavior. Paid automatic jobs and extra KV writes need an explicit operating allowance; the manual refresh path and personal guess statistics already work. No traction, interviews, partnerships or recorded videos have been invented.

Validation: 644 unit tests, 9 workerd tests, TypeScript, Wrangler dry run, dependency audit and browser smoke checks. Recorded September fixtures isolate historical presentation tests from changing live examples. Browser checks cover saved reading, address retention, Explore reload, historical board labels, watchlist, sharing and the long Unknown badge at 320px.
