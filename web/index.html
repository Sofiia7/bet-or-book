              What these terms mean
              Book
              Evidence of market-making inventory: meaningful buy and sell orders in this position's market. The grade counts corroborating signals, not a probability or a recommendation.
              Spot-covered short
              Matching spot at this address covers roughly the size of the short. It does not establish safety, shared collateral or the absence of off-chain debt.
              Perp / spot
              A perpetual contract tracks an asset's price; spot is the asset held directly.
              Net / gross exposure
              Net subtracts short dollars from long dollars; gross adds both. Different assets can still move independently.
              Resting orders / two-sided quoting
              Unfilled limit orders waiting to trade; bids and asks in the same market. Reduce-only exit orders do not count here.
              Fills / maker
              Executed trades. A maker supplies the resting order that another trader takes.
              Open interest
              The total value of outstanding perpetual contracts in a market.
              Main dex / HIP-3
              Hyperliquid's main perpetual exchange and independently deployed perpetual markets on Hyperliquid.
              Funding / cross leverage
              Periodic payments between longs and shorts; cross margin shares collateral between positions.
              First funder
              A wallet that transferred funds to this address. A transfer alone does not establish common ownership.

              Not visible to this tool: centralized exchanges, OTC deals and wallets with no on-chain link to this address.
